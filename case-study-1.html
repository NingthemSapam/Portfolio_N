Case Study 01

E-Commerce Listing Optimization & SEO Strategy

Transforming an unoptimized marketplace product page into a high-converting, keyword-rich storefront asset.

Role

E-Commerce Specialist

Platforms

Amazon / Shopify / eBay

Core Focus

Non-Technical SEO, Copywriting, UX

1. The Challenge & Background

A wireless noise-canceling headset listing on a major marketplace was underperforming in organic search traffic and sales conversions. The existing product listing suffered from low visibility, generic titles, and unstructured specifications that failed to highlight key selling points for remote workers and travelers.

2. Listing Transformation (Before vs. After)

❌ BEFORE (Unoptimized)

Product Title:

Black Wireless Noise Canceling Headphones with Mic

Description & Bullets:

These are good wireless headphones for work and travel. Has noise cancellation and a clear microphone for calls. Battery lasts a long time. Comes with a USB cable.

  • Good sound quality
  • Long battery life
  • Easy to connect
Key Flaws:
  • Missing high-intent search keywords (Bluetooth 5.3, Over-Ear, ANC)
  • No mobile-friendly formatting or bold callouts
  • Lacks clear benefits and technical specifications
✅ AFTER (Optimized)

Product Title:

Active Noise Canceling Wireless Headphones – Bluetooth 5.3 Over-Ear Headset with AI Clear Mic, 40-Hour Battery & Fast Charging – Ideal for Home Office & Remote Work

Optimized Bullet Points:

  • HYBRID ACTIVE NOISE CANCELLATION: Blocks up to 95% of ambient low-frequency noise (engine, traffic, office chatter), allowing complete focus during remote work calls or travel.
  • CRYSTAL-CLEAR HANDS-FREE CALLS: Equipped with dual AI-enhanced microphones and noise-canceling hardware compatibility to ensure your voice is crisp on Zoom, Teams, and mobile calls.
  • 40-HOUR PLAYTIME & FAST CHARGE: Enjoy up to 40 hours of continuous wireless playback on a single charge; a quick 5-minute charge delivers 3 full hours of listening time.
  • ALL-DAY ERGONOMIC COMFORT: Ultra-soft memory foam earcups and an adjustable headband provide a custom pressure-free fit for extended working sessions.
Target Keywords Embedded: Remote Work Headset Bluetooth 5.3 Over-Ear Active Noise Canceling

3. Strategic Breakdown

01. Non-Technical SEO Integration

Identified high-volume keywords related to remote work and audio equipment, seamlessly integrating them into the title, bullet points, and backend meta attributes to improve search indexing without sounding robotic.

02. Conversion-Driven Copywriting

Shifted the messaging from basic features to tangible customer benefits (e.g., converting *"Long battery life"* into *"40-Hour Playtime & 5-Min Fast Charge"*).

03. Mobile-First Readability

Implemented bold header tags in bullet points to allow shoppers on mobile devices to easily scan key product advantages within seconds.
