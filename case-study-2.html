E-Commerce Sales Analytics & Conversion Dashboard

Consolidating multi-channel sales data into actionable dashboard insights to reduce drop-offs and optimize ad spend.

Role

E-Commerce & Data Analyst

Tools Used

GA4, Power BI, Advanced Excel

Core Focus

Funnel Optimization & ROAS

Key Result

+22% Conversion Rate

1. The Problem

The client was running advertising campaigns across multiple platforms (Google Ads, Meta, Amazon PPC) but suffered from fragmented performance reporting. While web traffic was steady, sales conversions were declining, and team members were unable to identify where potential customers were abandoning their buyer journey.

2. Analytical Approach & Implementation

  • Data Aggregation: Extracted raw transactional data from Shopify, Amazon Central, and Google Analytics 4 (GA4) into Advanced Excel and Power BI data models.
  • Funnel Analysis: Mapped out the step-by-step conversion pipeline (Sessions → Product Views → Cart Adds → Checkout → Purchase) to isolate friction points.
  • SKU & Channel Segmentation: Categorized top-performing products vs. low-converting landing pages to reallocate advertising budgets effectively.

3. Key Insights Discovered

Friction Point Identified: High mobile drop-off between "Add to Cart" and "Checkout Initialization" due to slow script loading times on high-traffic mobile landing pages.
  • Wasted Ad Spend: 25% of ad budget was driving traffic to two low-inventory SKUs with below-average conversion rates (< 1%).
  • Peak Buying Hours: Converted orders peaked consistently between 7 PM - 10 PM, whereas PPC ad budgets were exhausting by midday.

4. Business Results & Impact

  • +22% Increase in Conversion Rate: Implemented checkout stream fixes based on funnel drop-off findings.
  • 15% PPC Cost Savings: Shifted budget schedules to peak buying hours and away from low-converting SKUs.
  • Automated Reporting: Replaced 5+ hours of manual weekly Excel reporting with a single auto-refreshing Power BI dashboard view.
